About Minddora AI

Minddora AI was built on a simple idea: students shouldn't have to manually dig through hundreds of pages of notes to find one answer. By combining semantic search with your own uploaded material, Minddora AI turns your notes into a searchable, conversational knowledge base — grounded entirely in what you've actually studied.

Our Mission

🔒

Privacy First
